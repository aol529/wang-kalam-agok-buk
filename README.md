# Wang' Kalam Agok Buk

A private personal site: journal, vision board, planner and, in time, an
inheritance of advice for my children. Static HTML/CSS/JS backed by Supabase.

- **Today**: the day's plan, Necessity, next steps, the journal prompt, on this day, the "Have a clear conscience" checklist, balance across the seven life areas, Days without: live timers for what you are giving up, with "I slipped" keeping the past streaks and the best one, a Pomodoro focus timer, and the Minimum Home Routine (daily, weekly and monthly ticks).
- **Vision**: goals as picture cards by life area, the hierarchy of needs, and the urgent/important quadrants. Career shows the 2018 Career notes (the working day); Wealth & Finance has the Desire to Riches form.
- **Planner**: each day in the time blocks of the working routine, with an evening review and the training mega-cycle. Tasks can be sized for the 1-3-5 rule, and one marked 🐸 as the frog to eat first.
- **Review**: the week, Monday to Sunday, from what the other tabs recorded: tasks and frogs per day, the clear conscience and home routine with "never miss twice" flagged, the life areas the week touched, journal moods and gratitude, Days without slips and the month's budget. "Write the weekly review" starts a journal entry with the numbers and five questions; next week's big rock goes on Monday as the frog. Today nudges to it on Sundays. No new SQL.
- **Budget**: the notebook page as four boxes: urgent + necessary, urgent − necessary, −urgent + necessary, −urgent − necessary (Covey's Necessity, Deception, Personal Leadership, Waste). Amounts in KES, monthly items ticked off month by month, one-offs once, with totals of what is paid and still to pay.
- **Wishlist**: the things I wish for on a notebook page: each with a rough price in KES, and under 🔗 a link to where to find it and a note. The total of what's on the list; tick a wish when it's yours and it moves down to Got, with the day.
- **Journal**: dated entries with mood, gratitude and life areas; any entry can be kept "for my children". The Stoic review starts a morning or evening entry from its questions, and "Blog it" turns an entry into a blog post.
- **Bible**: prayers and affirmations from "All I Want" (2014), then the Daily Bible Reading Plan (from github.com/aol529/bible-reading-plan) built into the tab: five plans, verse search, ticks and read-aloud. Unread days: from the day a plan was started (set by the first tick, or under the Today card), passed readings not ticked are counted under the Today card ("3 unread days", or "2 days behind" on the 90/60-day plans), listed, marked in the tables, and "Catch up" opens the oldest.
- **Library**: books to read, nothing tracked. A shelf; open a book for its contents by part and chapter, read a chapter and go on to the next, with a text size control. On the shelf, all public domain: *Seneca's Morals of a Happy Life, Benefits, Anger and Clemency* (L'Estrange, 1678), Marcus Aurelius's *Meditations* (Chrystal, 1902), Epictetus's *Enchiridion* (Higginson, 1865), *Discourses* (George Long, 1877) and *Golden Sayings* (Hastings Crossley, 1903), St George Stock's *A Guide to Stoicism*, Hyde's *The Five Great Philosophies of Life* (1911), *The Ethics of Aristotle* (Chase, 1847) and Arnold Bennett's *How to Live on 24 Hours a Day* (1908). Each book's text is a file in `js/library/`, loaded the first time it's opened; to add one, see the top of `js/library.js`. The blog post "Seneca: the man behind the morals" (`sql/10-blog-seneca.sql`) is the background.
- **Practice**: 21 Stoic exercises to do (prepare for the day, sort worries into in-my-power and not, test impressions, the view from above, rehearse the worst, voluntary discomfort, the evening review and more), each with how to do it in a few steps and a line from Seneca, Epictetus or Marcus in the Library's translations. "+ Today" puts one in today's plan; "Write it" starts a journal entry laid out for it; the morning and evening ones open the Stoic preparation and review, the retreat opens Breathe. One exercise is picked for each day, here and on Today. Nothing else is tracked.
- **Breathe**: guided breathing for meditation. Box breathing, 4-7-8, coherent breathing, the physiological sigh, the ten conscious breaths from the 2014 list, and Ānāpānasati (counting the out-breath). A circle grows and shrinks with the breath, with a count, a ring for the session, a soft tone and a buzz on phones. A finished session can tick "Meditate" on Today. Leaving the tab ends the session.
- **Sounds**: ambient sounds to mix for focus, rest and sleep: rain, storm, wind, waves, a stream, birds, a summer night, a coffee shop, the city, a train, a boat, a fireplace and more, plus white, pink and brown noise. Each has its own volume; ready mixes (Rainy café, Thunderstorm, Beach, Sleep…) or save your own; a timer fades out after 15 to 90 minutes. They keep playing on the other tabs, with a small player in the corner. Recordings are in `audio/` (seamless loops from Moodist's Pixabay/CC0 sounds; see `audio/CREDITS.txt`), downloaded the first time each is switched on; the noises are made in the browser. Mixes and volumes stay in this browser.
- **Systems**: six cheat sheets (burnout, memory, home, home routine, groceries, cooking) as action lists you tick off (ticks stay, so each card fills up as the system is set up), each read through the Four Laws of *Atomic Habits*; "+ Today" puts a step in today's plan, and "Things that make it easier" tick as you get them. The not-to-do list is Vision's Deception and Waste quadrants.
- **Blog**: posts in the seven life areas (or any category you add), draft or published. View reads them like a blog; Edit writes and manages them.
- **Gallery**: pictures with captions, categories and dates. View shows them as a wall; Edit adds, changes and deletes them. Blog covers come from here.
- **CMS**: Content edits the built-in text and lists (the affirmation, the clear conscience list, the home routine, the working day, quadrant examples, journal and letter prompts, The Way, the prayers, the Systems cards, the breathing techniques) as plain text, saved over the original with a reset back to it. Pages builds your own pages from blocks (heading, text, picture, checklist, quote, divider), each with an icon and a place in the menu. Menu sets the order of the sections, their group, and which are hidden. Public site switches on `public.html` with a title, tagline and about, and picks the published posts, pages and pictures it shows; nothing else can be read without signing in.
- **Legacy**: The Way (the life path explained), letters to open later, and the journal entries kept for the children. Prints as a book. "Download the archive" saves everything as one ZIP that needs no internet: book.html (The Way, letters, journal, vision, pages, blog and photos, printable to PDF), the same writing as .md files, the photos, and every table as JSON to restore from. Keep a copy on a drive once a year.

The menu groups the sections into **Do** (Today, Planner, Review, Systems, Budget), **Reflect** (Journal, Bible, Library, Practice, Breathe, Sounds) and **Keep** (Vision, Wishlist, Legacy, Blog, Gallery). On phones it is a dock along the bottom, with the rest, Account and Sign out under More. Icons are Lucide (ISC licence), inlined in `js/icons.js`.

The life path itself (areas, levels, virtues, quadrants, routine, prompts) lives in `js/framework.js`.

## Setup

1. Create a Supabase project.
2. In its SQL editor, run `sql/01-goals.sql`, then `sql/02-journal-planner.sql`, then `sql/03-blog-gallery.sql`, then `sql/04-clear-conscience.sql`, then `sql/05-timers.sql`, then `sql/06-systems.sql` (it also adds the blog post "Systems, not motivation"), then `sql/07-budget.sql`, then `sql/08-cms.sql` (run it again if you ever re-run 03, which takes away the public site's read access), then `sql/09-wishlist.sql`.
3. Authentication → Sign In / Providers: leave **Allow new users to sign up** on so people can create accounts on the site (each account sees only its own data); turn it off to keep the site to existing accounts. Authentication → URL Configuration: set **Site URL** to the live site and add `http://localhost:3001/` to **Redirect URLs**, so confirm-email and reset-password links come back here.
4. Put the project URL and anon key in `js/config.js` (Project Settings → API).
5. `npm start`, then open http://localhost:3001.

Pushing to `main` publishes the site to https://aol529.github.io/wang-kalam-agok-buk/ (`.github/workflows/pages.yml`).
